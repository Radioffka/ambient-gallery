Disconnect Google Photos

Disconnect Google Photos from Living Room TV?

When you disconnect:
• The ambient device registration for "Living Room TV" will be deleted from your Google Account.
• Photos and videos from Google Photos will no longer appear on this TV.
• Local access tokens and cached queue items will be securely purged.
• You can reconnect anytime by scanning a new setup QR code.
